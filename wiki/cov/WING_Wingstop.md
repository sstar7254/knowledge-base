### Company Overview

Wingstop Inc., together with its subsidiaries, franchises and operates restaurants under the Wingstop brand name. Its restaurants offer classic wings, boneless wings, and tenders that are cooked-to-order, and hand sauced-and-tossed in various flavors. As of December 26, 2020, the company had 1,506 franchised restaurants and 32 company-owned restaurants in 44 states and 10 countries worldwide. Wingstop Inc. was founded in 1994 and is based in Dallas, Texas.

### 2Q24 Earnings Summary

Wingstop's fiscal second quarter 2024 results display substantial growth in several critical metrics, indicating strong ongoing performance but also setting high expectations for future operations. Wingstop reported a ==28.7% increase in same-store sales growth==, primarily driven by transaction growth, which indicates robust consumer demand and effective brand expansion strategies.

The chain opened a record 73 new restaurants during the quarter, reflecting strong franchise partner confidence and effective market penetration strategies.

Internationally, Wingstop also sees robust growth opportunities, ==having experienced more than 82% growth in international average unit volumes (AUV) over two years.==
