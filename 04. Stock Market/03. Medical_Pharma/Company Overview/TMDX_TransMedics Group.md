### 기업 분석

- **Business Model Canvas**

    - **Value Proposition**
        
        **transforming organ transplant** therapy by **replacing an outdated preservation method** with innovative technology and an integrated service model, thereby significantly **increasing access to life-saving transplants and improving patient outcomes**
        
        - **Challenges in organ transplantation**
            
            1. Low deceased donor organ utilization: a large percentage of donor organs went unused
            
            1. Poor post-transplant clinical outcomes
            
            ==**⇒ Use cold static storage can solve 1) Severe time-dependent injury, 2) Limited access, 3) High complications.**==
            
        
        - **Organ Care System (OCS)**
            
            the only FDA-approved, portable, multi-organ, warm perfusion tech platform
            
            replicating many aspect of the organ’s natural living and functioning environment outside the human body
            
            |   |   |   |
            |---|---|---|
            |Capability (How it Works)|Value Proposition (Problem Solved)|Supporting Evidence|
            |**Warm, Oxygenated, Blood-based Perfusion**|**Minimizes ischemic damage**, decreasing time and distance limitations on transport. It allows for successful long-distance organ transport, which was previously challenging.|OCS Lung can extend preservation up to **24 hours**.|
            |**Ex-vivo Optimization and Treatment**|**Enables therapeutic optimization** of donor organs outside the body, significantly improving utilization and patient outcomes.|OCS Liver use resulted in a **43% reduction of severe post-transplant complications** and an **84% reduction in long-term biliary complications**.|
            |**Organ Viability Assessment**|**Allows for diagnostic evaluation** of the donor organ's suitability prior to transplantation, maximizing post-transplant outcomes.|OCS usage results in the **highest reported utilization** of deceased donor organs. OCS Liver has a utilization rate of **~97.5%** and OCS Heart **~97%**, compared to ~50% for NRP (Normothermic regional perfusion) in liver.|
            |**Increased Organ Utilization**|Provides additional access to organs, including historically unutilized organs from Donors after Circulatory Death (DCD) and extended criteria Donors after Brain Death (DBD).|OCS results in **87% utilization of historically unused lungs** and **81% utilization of historically unused DBD hearts**.|
            
        
        - **National OCS Program (NOP)**
            
            turnkey solution that provides outsourced organ retrieval, OCS perfusion mgmt, and transplant logistics services
            
            including efficiency and scalability for tranplant centers, Integrated logistics, shifting transplant timing (extended viability resulted in a “Day-Time Transplant”, which is a game changer by enabling multi-transplants on the sameday, driving resouce and financial efficiency for hospitals
            
        
    
    - **Customer Segment**
        
        - **Patients:** increased access to life-saving transplants, imporved clinical outcomes, better quality of life, quicker recovery
        
        - **Hospitals/Tranplant Centers:** increase transplant volume
        
        - **Insurers/Government:** cost-effective treatment
